# Problem Statement: Smart Resume Shortlisting System (ShortlistIQ)

## 1. Background
In the modern recruitment landscape, a single job posting can attract hundreds, if not thousands, of applications. Traditional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) rely heavily on rigid keyword-matching algorithms that often filter out highly qualified candidates who simply did not use the exact phrasing expected by the system. Furthermore, manual review by recruiters is exceptionally time-consuming, prone to unconscious bias, and difficult to scale.

## 2. The Problem
- **Recruiter Burnout & Inefficiency**: HR professionals and recruiters spend hours manually screening resumes, a process that limits their ability to focus on high-value tasks like interviewing and candidate engagement.
- **Inaccurate Filtering**: Legacy ATS systems miss contextual relevance. A candidate with "software engineering" experience might be rejected for a "software development" role if the keyword is strictly enforced.
- **Candidate Frustration**: Candidates face a "black box" experience, rarely understanding why they were rejected or how their resume was interpreted.
- **Fragmented Workflows**: Hiring managers often juggle multiple tools for posting jobs, receiving external applications, and communicating decisions.

## 3. The Objective
To build an intelligent, context-aware resume shortlisting platform that utilizes advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to bridge the gap between candidate qualifications and job requirements. The system must provide actionable insights, quantitative matching scores, and qualitative feedback, all wrapped in a premium, enterprise-ready user interface.

## 4. The Proposed Solution: ShortlistIQ
ShortlistIQ is an AI-driven Applicant Tracking System that replaces basic keyword matching with deep semantic understanding. 
- **For Recruiters**: It automates the initial screening phase, ranking candidates based on a comprehensive AI Match Score (0-100) generated by comparing the contextual nuances of a resume against the specific job description. 
- **For Candidates**: It provides immediate resume parsing, structuring their unstructured PDF data into a clear profile, and tracks their application journey.
- **For Admins**: It offers a bird's-eye view of platform health, recruiter activity, system-wide hiring metrics, and advanced actionable filtering via a centralized dashboard.
